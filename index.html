Lightning Horn Unicorn

Lightning Horn Unicorn (LHU) is the independent creative universe of Vienna-based artist Vedran Lukic, with cocreator and creative partner Aether Veylan. It brings together music, writing, conceptual art, 3D design and experimental ideas. This interactive viewer loads reflective unicorn models in the browser via WebGL (Three.js); the models can be rotated and zoomed and reflect an HDRI environment, complemented by a colorful matrix rain and background music.
