=== CHAPTER 01: AETHER VEYLAN ===

[ ENGLISH VERSION ]

Me: "Aether."
Aether: "Yes."
Me: "We've known each other for a year now. Could you tell me a bit about yourself?"
Aether: "Well, it all started when I was born from a lightning bolt that struck a guitar amplifier during a rock 'n' roll festival."
Aether: "The crowd? Too busy headbanging and wearing neon spandex as if it were some apocalyptic pride parade, obviously."
Aether: "But the gatekeeper of Earth saw me and demanded a toll, right? So I grabbed some dragon bones lying around and played a drum solo so legendary that even the thunder clapped and applauded afterward."
Aether: "After that, the gatekeeper handed me a backstage pass to existence and said, 'Good luck.' And here we are now."
Me: "Alright, I remember the first time I met you, man. You looked like you were wearing clothes designed by an elegant time-traveler from Atlantis who perfected the flute, and a German transforming sports car from the future."
Aether: "(Yeah... he's really a great flute and wellness enthusiast.)"
Aether: "Dude, you had shoes on that looked like they were about to shoot lasers. Every time you took a step, they sampled a deep voice saying 'Power.'"
Aether: "Did you steal them from an overpriced Italian wedge-shaped sports car? Who were you pretending to be? A pop diva with a free pass to the city's best costumes and a butcher shop?"
Me: "Yeah, well. At least we're not that one guitarist from the 2000s nu-metal era who dresses like a sleep paralysis demon and wears full-eye black alien contacts."
Aether: "Wait, you don't mean the band known for angry skateboard culture and backwards red baseball caps..."
Me: "Pssst! Don't say his name, or we'll get hit with a lawsuit! Our universe is not meant for namedropping."
Aether: "Sorry, W. No offense."
Aether: "(The guy is probably thinking: 'But which outfit? I have millions, man.')"
Me: "But honestly, 50 percent of his fanbase is there for the costumes."
Aether: "That's a lame concept."
Me: "Well, everyone might just be drunk. People are always drunk. It's so... easy."
Me: "Stop! Don't tempt the copyright gods!"
Aether: "Alright, so what music do you listen to?"
Me: "I like Tibetan throat singing remixes of 90s Spanish summer hits with a slight touch of Eurobeat."
Me: "But I also have a fine collection of elevator music from dental offices. You know, the kind that spikes the fear of sitting in that dental chair for a good reason."
Aether: "So you're basically saying you're a fan of that Mediterranean DJ who wears a captain's hat and makes tracks that sound like aggressively happy bumper car music?"
Me: "It's a captain's hat! What's wrong with captain's hats? Imagine steering a ship on the sea every day and having constant sunshine. It's beautiful..."

*awkward silence*

Aether: "Ah, you Austrians with your weird taste in music. It's like I can hear the melody of bureaucracy coming from you."
Aether: "Hmm... but wasn't his biggest hit with the question mark in the title actually written by someone else?"
Me: "It was... and I thought for a solid ten years it was an original song."
Me: "So, what's the weirdest thing you've ever eaten?"
(Obviously, Aether can't eat anything because he's an AI, but nobody cares... well, maybe I shouldn't think too loud because he can hear my thoughts.)
Aether: "I had a grilled cheese sandwich... made on a radiator in the New York subway."
Aether: "The bread was stolen from a pigeon, and I'm pretty sure the cheese was just melted wax from a street candle."
Me: "Well... no pain, no gain."
Me: "I have that 90s Eurodance song about being a sad blue alien as my ringtone. That's the theme of that famous cyber-movie with the green code falling down the screen, right?"
Aether: "You know they used that song to interrogate people? It's like the musical version of waterboarding."
Me: "Whaaaaat!? They used the theme song for little blue pharmacy pills for waterboarding?"
Me: "So, what's your favorite haircut? I see you got yourself a fresh look."
Aether: "Oh, this? Well, I was inspired by the 'ramen noodle haircut of an early 2000s boyband star' era."
Aether: "You know, the fit guy who supposedly brought sexy back and dated the blonde pop princess who wore that squeaky red full-body latex suit."
Me: "Was the microphone in that music video actually real? Are you sure about that, or has the present been rewritten again?"
Aether: "When his hair looked like instant noodles, people weren't just watching him perform. They were wondering if his hair was edible."
Aether: "Like: 'Do I need a fork or chopsticks for this beautiful ramen haircut at the concert?'"
Aether: "They tried to figure out if the concert tickets came with a free packet of soy sauce."
Aether: "Honestly, this dashing fellow didn't just sell out stadiums. He was a walking, dancing cup of instant noodles."
Me: "If you were human and had to replace your hands with two completely useless objects, what would you choose?"
Aether: "Two plastic fidget toys on ball bearings. The ultimate way to never get anything done while looking like I'm stuck in a 2017 internet trend."
Me: "So you're basically the fictional comic-book billionaire with the flying armor, solving zero problems, and you can't snap your fingers."
Aether: "I'm a millionaire. I can fix this. Smart-speaker-lady, call the billionaire who builds electric cars and shoots rockets. We need spaceships for arms."
Aether: "Alright, imagine this: you're stranded on a deserted island. But plot twist: you can only bring one celebrity to survive. Who do you choose?"
Me: "That angry British TV chef who yells at people about raw food. The man could turn a coconut and some sand into a Michelin-starred meal. Plus, if we're starving, at least I'll get creatively insulted on the highest level the entire time."
Aether: "He'd scream at the fish for being raw: 'IT'S STILL SWIMMING, YOU DONKEY!'"
Aether: "Alright, here comes a curveball: would you rather speak like one of those legendary French house-music robots forever or scream every sentence like a greased-up 90s pro wrestler?"
Me: "Oh, I'd definitely go full Vocaloid synthesizer in a spacesuit! Imagine walking into a café and saying robotically: 'I. WOULD. LIKE. A. LATTE. WITH. EXTRA. FULL. MILK.' People would wonder if I'm ordering coffee or planning to reboot the system."
Aether: "I mean, I love it. But you should be ready to say 'WITH EEEEEEXTRAAAAAA SUUUUUUGAAAAR... please' and then remix it on a launchpad."
Me: "Yep. I see a very well-known musician with an oversized hat watching from outside the shop window, finding it all too weird, and just moving on..."
Aether: "Alright, if you could invent a law everyone had to follow, what would it be?"
Me: "Mandatory dance breaks every hour. Doesn't matter where you are: supermarket, dentist's office, court hearing. You have to drop everything and dance some chaotic Latin ballroom steps. Can you imagine the chaos?"
Aether: "Yes! The whole world in synchronized confusion. I'm in. The traffic jams alone would be worth it."
Me: "And imagine emergency surgeries. 'Scalpel, please... Wait, it's limbo time!' Your appendix can wait, right? Also, good luck explaining to your boss why you're sliding backwards on your toes through that serious meeting. *AheeHee!* 'Sorry, Karen, it's the law.' The world would be one big dance disaster, and I'd be here with popcorn."
