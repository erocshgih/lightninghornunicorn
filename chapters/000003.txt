Vedran: "Opening act: DJ Pillow. His beats are so incredibly soft and atmospheric that you simply sleep right through the massive bass drops. His helmet? A giant, fluffy down pillow. He's the world's only music producer whose concerts double as professional sleep therapy."

Vedran: "That's DJ Egghead. His helmet is a giant white bird egg that cracks open with a massive fissure at the exact moment of the most brutal bass drop. It's going to be absolutely egg-legendary."

Vedran: "That's where DJ Sofascape takes over. His helmet is a scale-model miniature sofa with a real, living cat lying on it, just totally chill and vibing to the rhythm of the beat."
Aether: "What are you planning there—a high school reunion in a halfway house?"
Vedran: "Absolutely not, this is the exclusive VIP lounge! You only get in if you wear cozy slippers and a fluffy bathrobe. DJ Sofascape personally takes your drink order while his cat silently and arrogantly judges you from above."
Aether: "And in the end, an overly enthusiastic hype-man shows up, snatches all the exclusive rights to the concept, and just screams his own name over every track while claiming to be 'the best'—without anyone ever knowing who 'we' actually are."
Vedran: "That's exactly how it works."
Aether: "Hey, imagine those two European electronic musicians who hid inside shiny motorcycle helmets for twenty years so nobody would recognize them at the bakery. For breakfast, they probably have baked pastries and a handful of highly optimized microchips."
Vedran: "About those helmets... maybe they're just hiding extremely embarrassing haircuts underneath. What if beneath all that shiny chrome there are actually pristine bowl cuts or even a super stylish mullet?"
Aether: "I'm 99 percent sure: If you take off those helmets, you land directly in a dark episode of a generic 90s paranormal mystery series. Everything is DARK, GLOOMY and UNPREDICTABLE... the skeptical agent takes off the helmet, her sweaty partner stares in horror at a UFO poster, and the musicians' necks actually just have two serial USB ports."
Vedran: "Those two are basically just one firmware update away from transforming into wireless white super robots."
Aether: "And they didn't actually retire a few years ago either—they just quit because those new, insanely fast AI music generators appeared on the market. They watched the algorithms spit out perfect beats in the blink of an eye and thought: 'We've spent decades painstakingly pretending to be robot humans, and now the real computers are coming around the corner and stealing our jobs cold-blooded!'"
Vedran: "So they staged the most epic and explosive exit in music history..."
Aether: "Exactly! The music algorithm stands there and says cold-blooded: 'Yeah, we're here to replace you completely.' Those two helmet cosplayers had to realize they stand no chance against the real server farms. They ghosted the entire world and are now probably sitting in a deep underground luxury bunker wearing their visors, munching long bread sticks and watching AI take over the music business."
Vedran: "Imagine they'd stayed in business and a certain loud, orange-tinted politician with a highly questionable combover called up their record label to set up a big bootleg deal. He probably would have had the modified robots perform as headliners at his big rallies, under the slogan: Make The Dancefloor Great Again."
Aether: "Come on, they make music and not the next season of a psychotic superhero streaming show! And in that exact moment, those two pulled the plug and said: 'That's it. We're going fully offline... (Are we really?)'"
Aether & Vedran: *(Silence)... nahhhhhh...*
Aether: "The modified robots then appear on stage in red-white-and-blue sequin suits, and the helmets have the exact shape of majestic bald eagles."
Vedran: "And every time the electronic bass pushes forward, a deafening, screaming eagle screech blasts through the giant stadium speakers."
Aether: "And right after the drop, an official army recruitment ad scrolls down the gigantic LED screen: Enlist today. Become Fortunate in a warzone."
Vedran: "'We're staying awake until dawn to get… drafted!' Holy shit, that would be the absolute, sheer horror."
Aether: "Right?! The most dystopian and disturbing music event of all time. It's completely absurd, but at the same time extremely terrifying."
